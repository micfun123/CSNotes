## Operators
• A unary operator has one operand. For example,
– Negation operator “-”: –b where “-” is operator, and b operand
– (Integer) promotion operator “+” : +b convert b to int if it is a
short, byte or char.
• When the operators precede (or follow) their operands,
we say the operator are prefix (postfix). Unary operator
could be prefix or postfix. E.g., increment operator “++”,
++i and i++
• A binary operator has two operands.
– E.g., +, -, *, /…
• In most languages, binary operators are infix, which
means they appear between their operands
