
The follow control of programs, or execution sequence,
makes it possible to implement algorithms.
• The flow of control in programs can be examined at
several levels:
– Within expressions (governed by associativity and precedence
rules – last lecture on expressions),
– At the level of program statements (this lecture),
– At the level of program units (next lecture).
• Statements that provide capabilities such as selecting
among alternative control flow paths or causing the
repeated execution of certain collection of statements
are called control statements
